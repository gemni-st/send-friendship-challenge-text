You do not need an app, an account, or a plan. A friendship challenge works perfectly over plain text messages, and you can set one up in less time than it takes to order coffee.

The Five Minute Setup

Pick ten questions about yourself with short, definite answers. Number them one through ten. Text them to your friend in a single message with one rule: reply with all ten answers, no googling, no asking mutual friends. That is the entire setup.

Question Types That Work Over Text

Keep answers to a word or two so scoring is instant. Favorite snack. Birth month. Pet peeves. Dream travel destination. Go to emoji. The celebrity you would actually want at dinner. Text friendly questions keep momentum high and make it easy for busy friends to play between meetings.

Scoring Without the Awkwardness

Score it yourself and send back a simple tally with the correct answers marked. Add a one line comment on each miss, because the commentary is what makes it feel personal instead of clinical. Seven out of ten is a strong score. Anything above eight deserves public bragging rights.

Make It a Chain

The best part of a text challenge is how easily it spreads. When you send a friendship challenge to friends, ask each player to forward their own ten questions to someone else. One text can turn into a whole network of quizzes by the end of the day, and every link in the chain learns something new.

Why Text Beats Fancy Tools

No downloads, no logins, no learning curve. Your least tech savvy friend can play. Your busiest friend can answer between tasks. The humble text message remains the most inclusive gaming platform ever built, and it is already in everyone's pocket.
